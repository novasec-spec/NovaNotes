Nova was born from a simple idea: your digital life should be organized, beautiful, and personal. Not just another notes app — a complete ecosystem where your thoughts, memories, conversations, moods, AI companion, tasks, and life history all live together in harmony.

Built with ❤️ by novasec-spec, Nova is designed to be your second brain. Whether you're journaling, planning your day, chatting with loved ones, or just tracking your mood — Nova is there for you.
